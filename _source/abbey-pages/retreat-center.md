#### Retreat & Conference

#### Center

St. Bernard Retreat & Conference Center is a two storied building containing forty-six rooms. Each room contains two twin beds, private bath, telephone, and individual room temperature control. Two handicap-accessible rooms are available. Each contain one queen-size bed, private bath, telephone, and individual room temperature  control.  The Retreat Center has two meeting rooms:  one large meeting room that seats up to 80 people & one small meeting room that seats up to 16.


To learn more about the Retreat and Conference center, [please fill out our Reservation Inquiry Form](https://www.stbernardabbey.com/reservations) or call our Retreat Center Staff at 256-734-3946.


We no longer have rooms in the monastery available for guests.
