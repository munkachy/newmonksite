#### create a will

#### (for free!)


As a service to friends and alumni, we now offer a free on-line Will creation tool. We have subscribed to My Catholic Will, a trusted third party provider, who works with Catholic organizations throughout the US.  (But you don’t need to be Catholic to use it!)

The tool will take you step-by-step through the Will creation process, allowing you to name spouse, children, grandchildren, friends and charities as beneficiaries of your estate.


It’s free, easy, quick, and confidential. Just answer the questions, then follow the directive at the end.​

[Create a Will](http://mycatholicwill.com/stbernardabbey)
