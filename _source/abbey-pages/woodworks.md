# St. Bernard Abbey Woodworks

St. Bernard Abbey Woodworking is a ministry of the Benedictine monks of St. Bernard Abbey in Cullman, Alabama. We are a team of skilled craftsmen who use our talents and experience to create beautiful and durable wood products for churches, homes, and businesses. Contact [carpenter@stbernardabbey.com](mailto:carpenter@stbernardabbey.com) to learn more or place an order.

We specialize in custom-made furniture, altars, pulpits, pews, doors, cabinets, and more. We also offer restoration and refinishing services for antique and damaged wood items. Whether you need a new piece of furniture or want to preserve an old one, we can help you with your woodworking needs.

Our work is inspired by our faith and our monastic tradition, which dates back to the 6th century. We follow the Rule of St. Benedict, which teaches us to pray and work in harmony with God and one another. We strive to honor God in everything we do, and to serve our customers with excellence and integrity.

We invite you to browse the images below and see some of our past projects. You can also contact us for a free consultation and estimate. We would love to hear from you and discuss how we can help you with your woodworking project. Thank you for visiting St. Bernard Abbey Woodworking. May God bless you and your loved ones.
