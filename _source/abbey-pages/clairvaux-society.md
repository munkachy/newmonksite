#### Clairvaux Society

Named after St. Bernard of Clairvaux, the famous eleventh-century French abbot and doctor of the Church, the Clairvaux Society honors donors who remember St. Bernard through their Will or a variety of planned gifts.  The Clairvaux Society Foundation is an endowed fund—only earnings from the fund are used to make possible the work of the Abbey and Prep School.

Members of the Society are invited to attend the annual Clairvaux Society Dinner.  It’s an opportunity to induct new members, enjoy fellowship, and honor and thank Society members for their visionary leadership, philanthropy, and generosity.


For questions or to learn more about the Clairvaux Society, contact the St. Bernard Development Office at (256) 739-6682 or [rgongre@stbernardprep.com](mailto:rgongre@stbernardprep.com).  All inquiries remain confidential.
