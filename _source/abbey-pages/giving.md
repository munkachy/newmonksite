#### Giving

The monks rely on the generosity of many to help support their life of prayer to God and service to the people of North Alabama.  There are many ways that you can assist the monks in their life and mission.  Click below to learn more.


- [Donate Make a one-time or recurring donation to support the monks of St. Bernard Abbey.](https://www.stbernardabbey.com/donate-now)
- [Planned Giving Become part of our Clairvaux Society to remember St. Bernard through wills, planned gifts, and estate plans of any size.  Planned gifts help ensure our stability and future ministry.](https://www.stbernardabbey.com/clairvaux-society)
- [Festivals](https://www.bloominfestival.com/) Join us for our two annual festivals, the [Bloomin' Festival](https://www.bloominfestival.com/) and [Bernard Blues & BBQ](https://stbernardprep.com/bernard-blues-bbq/).  Monks don't throw parties often, but when they do, everyone is invited!
- [Contact Us Get in touch with us to learn more about how you can help the monks in their mission.](https://www.stbernardabbey.com/giving)
