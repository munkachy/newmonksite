#### Mass Intentions

It is a longstanding tradition in the Church that a Priest may be requested to offer a Mass for a specific intention.  You may request that Masses be offered by the monk-priests of St. Bernard for your intentions.

##### Stipends

You are invited to make a free will offering, called a stipend, to apply the Mass to a specific intention. Your offerings for Holy Masses are used to support the monks of Saint Bernard Abbey.


Suggested stipends/ offerings:

- Individual Mass: $10.00 (minimum), $15.00, $25.00
- Novena of Masses (9 consecutive Masses): $90 (minimum)

##### ​

##### FAQs

- How much does a Mass cost? The stipend in no way corresponds with the value of a Mass, which is of infinite value. Mass does not cost anything.  Rather, the asker gives a free-will offering to support the work of the priest celebrating the Mass.
- Can you offer Mass on a specific date for my intention? You are welcome to request a specific date and we will try to accommodate your request, but it is almost impossible for us to guarantee that a Mass will be offered on a specific date.
- How do I request a Mass to be said? Please send a letter indicating: +  Your name +  The Intention of the Mass (including, if applicable, the name of any person(s) for whom the intention is associated and whether the person is living or dead.) +  The Stipend for the Mass (see above recommended offering).      Cash or check made out to "St. Bernard Abbey".  USD only.      If you are unable to make a donation at this time, you may           still submit a request. +  Mail your request to:
