## Open House & Dedication of

## Monastery Building

Abbot Marcus and the Monks invite you to the Dedication of their newly renovated monastery building on ​Friday, June 27, 2025!

​​

- Tours of the monastery cloister will be available from 11 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
- Solemn Vespers in the Abbey Church and Dedication of the Monastery at 5 p.m.
- Festive Dinner at 6 p.m. in the Abbey Byre

COST:  Free.  Please be our guests.

## ​​

Please RSVP for the Monastery Open House and Dedication using the form below.

Note: The Dedication on Friday, June 27, is free, but the fee for the Alumni Reunion on Saturday, June 28 is $100.
