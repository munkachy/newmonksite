#### Reservation Inquiry

If you are interested in visiting St. Bernard Abbey overnight or staying in our Retreat and Conference center, either individually or as part of a group, please get in touch with our Retreat Center Staff by using the form below or calling 256-734-3946.  While we cannot guarantee reservations online, we invite you to suggest recommended dates for your visit.


Please note that we do not offer directed retreats. The monks are unavailable to lead retreats, though they may be able to hear confessions.


Only two persons allowed per room. Each room has two twin sized beds. There are no cribs, pack-and-plays, or cots available.
