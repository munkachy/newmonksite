Note on the statue pictures: the photographs are public domain, but the statue is a recent sculpture. Sources name Ira Chaffin as the sculptor and give 2007 or 2009 as the date. In the US a photo of a copyrighted sculpture can be restricted by the sculptor's copyright (see Commons:FOP US). Ask the Grotto or abbey whether the abbey holds the statue's rights before using it commercially. For the abbey's own site this is almost certainly fine.
