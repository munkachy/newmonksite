# Anne Stephens's SmugMug: catalogue

Source: https://annestephens.smugmug.com/ (Anne Stephens, a friend of the abbey). Permission: the abbey's webmaster (Br. Augustine), 1 Nov 2026, for the new abbey website, credited **"Photo: Anne Stephens"**.

Fetched 1 Nov 2026. Everything here is publicly viewable (no gallery is password-protected; downloads are allowed but the site's largest public sizes were used, not originals). Files are SmugMug's X4Large size (2048 px long side; a few smaller where that is the largest the site offers), JPEG, recompressed to quality 82 where over 1.5 MB. Total 417 files, about 410 MB, in `_source/stephens/<gallery-slug>/`. File names are `<original file name>-<SmugMug image key>.jpg`; the image key finds the photo again on SmugMug.

No photo on the site has a caption, title or keywords, so names of monks below come from looking at the pictures, compared with the named headshots in *Monk Headshots* (file names give the names) and the portraits in `_source/abbey-img/` (monk-profiles-*.jpg). "Likely" = good match; "possibly"/"unsure" = check with Br. Augustine before captioning. Novice Br. Gabriel Maddox has left: don't use photos of him (2026 novitiate gallery). Br. Joseph Goode and Br. Jude are no longer at the abbey.

Selection: ~16,700 photos in 61 galleries. Every gallery was sampled (all photos of the smaller "places/people/work" galleries, 32 evenly spaced photos of each big event gallery, 80 of the two June 2024 galleries) and the best were taken. More photos of any event can be fetched from the gallery URLs. Not downloaded: Fr. Paschal's Mass at the Cathedral of St. Paul (not the abbey), most of the near-duplicate burst shots, guest/family group photos.
