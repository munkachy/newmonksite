# Craftsman monks of St. Bernard Abbey: public sources only

Research of 1 Nov 2026. Everything here comes from published sources, cited inline.
**The fuller notes** (from the abbey's own Necrology, with Br. Joseph's own words and about twenty more craftsman
monks) are kept privately in the `munkachy/psalter` repo, `notes/abbey-site/CRAFTSMEN.md`, next to the Necrology
itself. The Necrology is not public: ask the abbot before quoting it on the website.

## Br. Joseph Zoettl, OSB (1878–1961), builder of the Ave Maria Grotto

- Born Michael Zoettl in Landshut, Bavaria; came to St. Bernard in 1892, aged about 14, meaning to become a priest.
  A back injury (a hunched back) kept him from ordination; he became a lay brother (vows 1897). Sources: NRHP
  nomination (1983, NPGallery); Encyclopedia of Alabama, "Ave Maria Grotto" (encyclopediaofalabama.org).
- Kept house for Benedictine priests in the missions of the Southeast for several years (NRHP).
- From about 1910 he ran the abbey powerhouse, long days, for some thirty years (NRHP; SPACES archive).
- Made small grottoes for sale, then miniature buildings ("Little Jerusalem") on the brothers' recreation ground,
  from about 1912–1918 (sources differ: NRHP, EOA, Grotto site).
- In 1932–34 the miniatures were moved into the abbey's old quarry; the Ave Maria Grotto was dedicated on
  **17 May 1934** (NRHP; EOA; avemariagrotto.com).
- Built from cement, stone, marble, shells, glass, tiles, cold-cream jars, marbles, toilet floats and whatever
  people sent him (EOA; Grotto guide). About 125 miniatures in the Grotto (Grotto site; some sources say ~150 works).
- Last work: the Basilica of Lourdes, 1958. Died **15 October 1961**; buried in the abbey cemetery, about 100 yards
  north of the Grotto gift shop (Grotto site; EOA).
- The Grotto is on the National Register of Historic Places (1984).
- Bronze statue of Br. Joseph at the Grotto by sculptor Ira Chaffin (2007 or 2009; check).

**Photos of Br. Joseph himself**: none is public domain or CC. Known holders (ask permission): Alabama Department of
Archives and History, Troy University (postcard, ~2100×3270), Auburn University; the Grotto itself. Public-domain
photos of the Grotto (Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress) are in `research-img/` with `CREDITS.md`.

## Br. Jude Johnston, OSB (1933–2025), sculptor, artist, engineer

- Born Henry Odie Johnston Jr., Florence, Ala., 19 Jan 1933; St. Bernard Prep class of 1951; professed 1954;
  the abbey's last lay brother. Died 19 Jun 2025.
- Made habits and vestments in the tailor shop; studied ceramics with Fr. Timothy Harrison; apprenticed by Abbot Bede
  to the sculptor Herbert Jogerst, who carved the statues of the Abbey Church. Carved a bust of Br. Joseph Zoettl
  ("my first awkward attempt at portraiture"), still in the Grotto Shop, and the Dove of the Holy Spirit over the
  Chapter Room door. Studied and taught sculpture, photography, engineering and design (Notre Dame, Auburn, Yale).
- Worked under the name "Nov Ontos"; his collection went to the Evelyn Burrow Museum, Wallace State, which showed it
  in 2026 ("a true polymath").
- Sources: Cullman Tribune obituary, 23 Jun 2025 (cullmantribune.com/2025/06/23/obituary-henry-odie-johnston-jr/);
  One Voice (onevoicebhm.org/last-lay-brother-st-bernard-abbey-peacefully-goes-home-lord);
  Wallace State (wallacestate.edu/news/2026/04/01/jude-johnston_exhibit.html).

## Others with public sources

- **Fr. Timothy Harrison** (1921–1982), painter, sculptor, potter: made the Statue of Christ at the Grotto
  (Grotto self-guide); taught Br. Jude ceramics (Br. Jude obituary).
- **Herbert Jogerst** (layman, sculptor): the statues of the Abbey Church (Br. Jude obituary).
- **Fr. Aloysius Plaisance** (1920–1992), writer; Grotto director; his books are reprinted by the abbey's
  **St. Bernard Scriptorium** (avemariagrotto.com/store/c14/St._Bernard_Scriptorium.html).
- **Abbot Marcus Voss**: published a four-volume Book of Hours for the monastic choir (Cullman Times).
- **Today**: the Benedictine Brothers' Bakery (Br. Benedict Dyar, head baker; Fr. Pachomius formerly), St. Bernard
  Abbey Woodworks ("custom-made furniture, altars, pulpits, pews, doors, cabinets"), the Scriptorium, the farm,
  candle making, tailoring (stbernardabbey.com).

## Names found only in the Necrology (private; to confirm with the community before publishing)

Woodworkers and builders: Br. Gerard Staudinger (carpenter; Abbey Church pews), Br. Nicholas Weiss (cabinet maker and
woodcarver; Altar of Repose), Fr. Jerome Lawrence (built the old gym), Fr. Placidus Becker (painter; landscaping),
Fr. Bonaventure Winklmann (drafting). Scribes and printers: Fr. Edward Stoeberl (illuminated choir books),
Fr. Charles Fries (printing press). Others: Br. Vincent Kiefl (glazier), Br. Edmund Benziger (shoemaker),
Br. Wolfgang Wolf (tailor; botanist), Br. Aemilian Kopps (gardener; rosaries), Br. Francis Rotkopf (boilers, plant).
The "Br. Nicholas" who drew the campus map for the Bloomin' Festival is a different, living monk: ask who.
