## General.zip: the archives' working files

- `data bases/Record of St Bernard Monks.xlsx`: every monk (1,460 rows): names, monastic name, birth, home, profession,
  solemn vows, ordination, abbatial election, death, assignment. **Personal data: keep out of the repo.**
- `data bases/Monastic Dates for SBA Monks.xlsx`: the current community (positions, birthdays, profession, ordination).
- `data bases/Archives card catalog.xlsx` (and -search, .xlsm): 3,040 entries: shelf/drawer/box, subject, title, author,
  date, source. The finding aid for the physical archive.
- `catagories for files.xlsx`, `monk name files.pptx`, `archives logo.png`, `room updates/` (cabinet orders and a proposed
  archives room), `Kentucky info.pptx/.pdf` (the abbey's Kentucky missions), `Research on Fr Baasen.docx`, a time card.

Leads: the Kentucky missions (Abbot Ambrose Reger, St. Camillus Academy, Corbin) and Fr. Baasen could become history
pieces; the card catalog could answer questions about any monk or event.
