Pilgrims to the Grotto and the Shrine of the Most Blessed Sacrament (Hanceville). "We no longer have rooms in the monastery available for guests."

Community: "The 30 men who make up the monastery community"; "more than 800 acres"; "an increase of vocations that makes it unique among most American Benedictine monasteries".
History: monks from Metten Abbey (c. 700) → St. Vincent Archabbey (Latrobe, 1840s) → Alabama 1870s → St. Bernard Abbey 1891 → school 1892. 1892–1979 high school, junior college, four-year college, seminary at overlapping intervals; St. Bernard Prep opened 1984 (grades 7–12, boarding + day). 1934 Grotto dedicated. 1963 monastery built. 1981 Retreat Center. 27 Jun 2025 renovated monastery dedicated. American-Cassinese Congregation; Diocese of Birmingham.
Abbots: Victor Clark; 9th Cletus (May 1995–Jul 2019); 10th Marcus Voss (Jul 2019–Oct 2025); 11th Brendan J. Seipel, elected and confirmed 29 Jul 2026 (from Lanesville, Indiana; entered 2002; vows 21 Mar 2004; Guest Master, Retreat Center director, Abbey Farm director, Subprior, Prior); "eleventh Abbot in St. Bernard's 135-year history".
