## What is public and what isn't

The portfolio lists 54 galleries. Only **13 open without logging in**. The other 41 send you to a "Login to the
gallery" page. Those weren't opened. For a gated gallery, the only public image is the cover picture shown on the
portfolio page, served at 2400 px.

- Public gallery photos: the largest size the viewer serves is `lowres`, **1600 px on the long side**. The `hires` size
  is only available through the download button, which goes through Pic-Time's download policy, so it wasn't used.
  The photos have no watermark.
- Portfolio covers: served at 2400 px. These were saved for the abbey galleries, including the gated ones.
- To get the full gated galleries, or 2400 px versions, ask Josh for the gallery links or the files. They are
  most likely the galleries he shot for the abbey: Abbot Brendan, Fr. Pachomius, Br. Augustine's profession, and
  MonkMindset.

### `abbot-marcus-thanksgiving-mass/`: Mass of Thanksgiving for Abbot Marcus Voss, 7 Feb 2026 (abbey church)

137 photos, `viola-marcus-001.jpg` … `-137.jpg` (1600 px, in gallery order), plus `viola-marcus-cover.jpg`
(2400 px, the gallery's cover: black-and-white close-up of the mitred abbot beside a carved statue, like 110/111).

**Who's who.** The honoree, in a monastic chasuble with an abbot's zucchetto, is taken to be **Abbot Marcus Voss**
(white hair, glasses). He is almost certainly the celebrant. He is the man wearing the mitre in 113–122 and preaching
in 095–107. In the reception (124–137), the tall white-haired monk in a black habit is very likely Abbot Marcus too.
The bishop (pink zucchetto, 024, 046, 050) and the second bishop with crozier (112, 118–122) are not identified.
The other monks can't be named from these photos: unsure.

### `abbot-brendan-acolyte/` (gated gallery, cover only)

- ★`viola-brendan-acolyte-cover.jpg` (2400 × 1600): the abbey church. A monk in choir dress, a priest in a green
  chasuble, and a young acolyte in cassock and surplice, with the abbey's arms on a vestment. From the gallery name,
  this is Br. Brendan Seipel being instituted acolyte. **Which figure is he: unsure** (most likely the white-haired
  monk in a white cowl beside the acolyte).

### `fr-pachomius/` (gated gallery, cover only)

- ★`viola-pachomius-cover.jpg` (2400 × 1600): a priest seen from behind in a purple chasuble with the Chi-Rho,
  praying at a small altar with a crucifix, two candles and the Missal, against the abbey's stone wall. Presumably
  **Fr. Pachomius Alvarado** (gallery title; face not seen).

### `solemn-profession-br-augustine/` (gated gallery, cover only)

- ★`viola-augustine-profession-cover.jpg` (1600 × 2400): the abbey church in warm light through the high windows.
  A monk in black habit stands on the carpet before the stained-glass door; others watch in the foreground. This is
  **Br. Augustine Munkachy's** solemn profession (2025). The standing monk is likely Br. Augustine (unsure).

### `monkmindset/` (gated gallery, cover only)

- ★`viola-monkmindset-cover.jpg` (2400 × 1600): four monks in black habits in wooden choir stalls, reading,
  in a dim chapel. The place is **unsure**: it may be the abbey's chapel. The monks can't be named: unsure.
