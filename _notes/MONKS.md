# Get to know the monks: source material

Gathered 1 Nov 2026 for the "Get to know the monks" section. Sections follow `ROSTER.md`. Br. Joseph Goode (#19) and
Novice Br. Gabriel Maddox (#25) are left out at the webmaster's request: one has gone to another monastery, the other has
left the abbey.

## What could and could not be read

- **The abbey's Facebook page could not be read.** Every route (desktop and phone user agents in Playwright, m., mbasic.,
  the page-plugin embed and curl) redirected to Facebook's login page before any post loaded. Search engines index none of
  its posts. The only text found is a search-engine snippet of the page: "Abbatial Blessing of Abbot Brendan on November
  21!" (no year shown; https://www.facebook.com/stbernardabbey/). **No Facebook post is quoted below.** To use the posts,
  someone logged in to Facebook (e.g. Br. Augustine) will have to copy them out or export them.
- What *is* the abbey's own words: the **Monk Profiles** page on the abbey's website (first person, written for the abbey),
  the abbey's announcements on its home page, and press releases the abbey sent to the Cullman papers ("Special to the
  Times", "St. Bernard Special to The Times", or photos credited "(St. Bernard)"). These are marked **[abbey]**.
- **Feast days:** a source states which saint a monk is named for only in Br. Augustine's case. The others are left blank
  rather than guessed. Ask each monk, or the webmaster, for his patron (several names have more than one saint).

## 1. Abbot Brendan Seipel, O.S.B. (Br. Prior Brendan on the roster; 11th Abbot)

- **Hometown:** Lanesville, Indiana (Abbey home [abbey]).
- **Entered:** 2002 (Abbey home [abbey]).
- **Vows:** professed vows as a Benedictine monk on 21 March 2004 (Abbey home [abbey]).
- **Election:** elected and confirmed 11th Abbot on 29 July 2026. The election opened with the Mass of the Holy Spirit on 15
  June 2026 and was recessed "until canonical recourse could be made to the Holy See." "Abbot Brendan becomes the eleventh
  Abbot in St. Bernard's 135-year history." His formal blessing was to follow "in the coming months" (Abbey home [abbey]).
  The Facebook snippet gives the Abbatial Blessing as 21 November (see above; year not shown).
- **Work:** "Guest Master, Director of the St. Boniface Retreat Center, Director of the Abbey Farm, member of the Senior
  Council, and as Subprior and Prior" (Abbey home [abbey]). Named Temporary Administrator when Abbot Marcus resigned on 17 Oct
  2025 (https://cullmantimes.com/2025/10/17/abbot-marcus-j-voss-o-s-b-resigns-as-abbot-of-st-bernard-abbey/). Chairman of the
  board of St. Bernard Prep in May 2026 (Tribune 2026 leadership).
- **His words** (as chairman, May 2026): "SBP's future is full of hope as we continue our mission of cultivating a love of
  learning and a life of virtue in young men and women" (Tribune 2026 leadership).
- **Other:** in 2013 a cyclist riding for the American Cancer Society was hit by a car near Falkville. He and his wife thanked
  "especially the Doyle's and Br. Brendan Seipel at St. Bernard" for their kindness
  (https://stbernardprep.blogspot.com/2013/09/brain-cancersurvivor-michael-mcbride.html).
- **Feast day:** not stated.
- **Photo:** monk-img/brendan-1.jpg.

## 2. Rt. Rev. Cletus D. Meagher, O.S.B. (9th Abbot)

- **Abbey profile, in his own words [abbey]:**
  > Hello, I'm Abbot Cletus. I was born in Cleveland, Tennessee in 1944. I was educated by the Nashville Dominican Sisters at
  > Notre Dame Grammar School in Chattanooga, TN. In 1958 entered St. Bernard Preparatory Seminary and graduated in 1962, and
  > entered St. Bernard Abbey in 1964; I was ordained a priest in 1971.
  >
  > As a monk, I have served the monastic community in various capacities (Subprior, Prior, Novice Master, Procurator). I
  > have taught in our schools and was also pastor to Sacred Heart Parish in Cullman from 1986 to 1995. I was elected Ninth
  > Abbot of St. Bernard Abbey in May 1995 and served in that capacity until retirement from that Office in July 2019. I
  > currently serve as Pastor of St. Michael Catholic Church in St. Florian, Alabama. St. Michael Church is the first mission
  > the monks of St. Bernard Abbey established when they came to Alabama.
- **Born:** 15 November 1944, Cleveland, Tennessee, "the fifth of six children of Oscar A. and Mildred (Sommers) Meagher"
  (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]).
- **Entered / vows / ordination:** entered as a novice 1964. Simple vows 4 July 1965
  (https://www.cullmantribune.com/2019/02/12/st-bernards-abbot-cletus-d-meagher-set-to-retire-this-year/). Ordained priest
  24 May 1971 (same). Elected abbot 8 May 1995 (same).
- **Education:** St. Bernard Prep Seminary, graduating in 1962 ("last graduation class of St. Bernard Prep until
  re-establishment in 1984"). BA in Philosophy 1967 (minors in Psychology and English). M.Div., St. Louis University, 1971
  (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]). He also studied theology at St. Bede Abbey, Peru, Illinois (Tribune 2019).
- **Work:** taught theology at St. Bernard College / Southern Benedictine College 1971–79. Chaplain at Sacred Heart Monastery.
  Campus ministry. Pastor of Sacred Heart, Cullman, 1986–95. Diocesan Presbyteral Council. Spiritual director and confessor in
  retirement. Pastor of St. Michael, St. Florian, from October 2021 (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]). Farm manager earlier (Tribune
  2019). School chaplain of St. Bernard Prep from 2025–26
  (https://www.cullmantribune.com/2025/06/06/st-bernard-prep-announce-academic-leadership-roles-new-act-prep-for-2025-2026/).
- **His words:** "I look forward to the election of the new abbot and transitioning into a new role of service for the
  community – whatever that might be" (Tribune 2019). On first vows: "We give everything away and our whole being belongs to
  God and to the community of brothers among whom we work and pray as we strive towards the heavenly Kingdom" (Times 2015).
- **Jubilees:** Golden Jubilee of profession and priesthood, Silver Jubilee as abbot (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]).
- **Feast day:** not stated.
- **Photos:** monk-img/cletus-1.jpg, cletus-2.jpg.

## 3. Rt. Rev. Marcus J. Voss, O.S.B. (10th Abbot)

- **Abbey profile, in his own words [abbey]:**
  > I was born and raised in Cullman, Alabama. The monks from St. Bernard Abbey staffed Sacred Heart Parish where I grew up.
  > In addition, Abbot Victor Clark, O.S.B., a monk of St. Bernard Abbey, is my uncle, so St. Bernard was part of our family
  > from the very beginning of my life.
  >
  > I enrolled at St. Bernard High School as a freshman. After finishing high school, I was accepted at the University of
  > Alabama for college. While going through orientation, I decided to "try my vocation" as a member of the St. Bernard
  > community. I entered the Seminary at St. Bernard in the fall of 1961 and after two years of study, I entered the
  > monastery. I made vows as a Benedictine monk of St. Bernard Abbey in 1964 and was ordained a priest in 1971. I have spent
  > my whole life at St. Bernard Abbey taking various assignments in our monastery and schools. I served as the 10th Abbot of
  > St. Bernard Abbey, from July 2019 to October 2025.
- **Born:** 29 Aug 1943, Cullman, to Joe and Catherine Voss. Siblings Martha Thomas, Marcella Bailey, Michael Voss
  (https://www.cullmantribune.com/2019/07/24/updated-father-marcus-voss-elected-10th-abbot-of-saint-bernard-abbey/).
- **Vows / ordination:** vows 5 July 1964. Ordained 24 May 1971 with Abbot Cletus (same; also
  https://cullmantimes.com/2024/10/06/st-bernard-abbey-celebrates-life-of-the-right-reverend-marcus-j-voss-o-s-b/ [abbey,
  "St. Bernard Special to The Times"]).
- **Abbot:** elected 24 July 2019. Resigned 17 Oct 2025 "for health reasons"
  (https://cullmantimes.com/2025/10/17/abbot-marcus-j-voss-o-s-b-resigns-as-abbot-of-st-bernard-abbey/). Retirement Mass
  6 Feb 2026 (https://cullmantimes.com/2026/02/11/retirement-celebration-honors-abbot-marcus-voss/).
- **Work** (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]): Abbey Choir Master 1971–91. "Published a four-volume 'Book of Hours' for use in the
  monastic choir." Sub-Prior 1977–82. Registrar of St. Bernard College / Southern Benedictine College 1970–78. Director of the
  Abbey Formation Program 1979–86. Business manager of the abbey 1995–2008. Visitator of the American-Cassinese Congregation
  from 1988. Headmaster of St. Bernard Prep 1986–92, President 1992–2011, Development Director 1992–2019. Weekend ministry
  around Alabama 1971–2019. He was also college registrar, abbey procurator, choirmaster, novice master and junior master, and
  played "an instrumental role in the re-establishment of St. Bernard Preparatory School" in the 1980s (Tribune 2019).
- **Education:** St. Bernard College 1961–66, summa cum laude, BA Philosophy. M.Div., St. Louis University, 1970. MA in
  Spirituality, Creighton, 1981 (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]). Theology also at St. Bede Abbey, Peru, Ill. Summer monastic studies
  at Sant'Anselmo, Rome (Tribune 2019).
- **Other:** a basketball standout. Cullman County Sports Hall of Fame 2008 (Tribune 2019).
- **His words:** "I find taking this position is a tremendous honor and very humbling … I look forward to continuing the great
  tradition of St. Bernard in the community" (https://cullmantimes.com/2019/07/25/abbot-marcus-cullman-native-follows-family-tradition-as-new-abbot-of-st-bernard/).
  Recalling Fr. Lambert at age 19: "I told him I was thinking about entering the seminary, and he replied, 'What took you so
  long?'" (Times, Feb 2026).
- **Feast day:** not stated.
- **Photos:** monk-img/marcus-1.jpg, marcus-2.jpg.

## 4. Fr. Joel Martin, O.S.B., Subprior

- **Abbey profile, in his own words [abbey]:**
  > Born in the small South Georgia town of Fitzgerald, I am the first of four children of Jack and Anne Martin. After
  > attending public schools in Fitzgerald I went on to graduate from Emory College in Atlanta. I became a Catholic in 1976
  > and entered the monastery in 1984. I applied to the monastery in 1981 but "chickened out" when it was time to join. My
  > motivation was to give myself totally and radically to God; that idea made me happy and frightened at the same time. When
  > I finally got to the monastery in September of 1984 I was surprised that, though the life was very challenging, God's
  > grace allowed me to fit.
  >
  > Since being in the monastery I've served in various ways: choirmaster, novice master, vocation director and headmaster and
  > president of our school. St. Bernard is no place for thumb-twiddling. Plus I have the awesome opportunity to love and serve
  > God in a community of brothers – and hopefully make a good difference in the world.
- **Ordination:** priest in 1991. He studied at St. Meinrad (MA Theology, M.Div.) (Tribune 2026 leadership).
- **Work:** at St. Bernard Prep as "teacher, headmaster and president since the fall of that year" (1991). In the monastery:
  "sub-prior, novice master, choirmaster and vocation director". Interim headmaster from 2026–27 (Tribune 2026 leadership).
  Junior master, presenting monks for vows (Times 2014; Cullman Times, Sept 2025). On a trip to Colombia, recruiting for the
  prep school, he met the future Fr. Pachomius at the monastery of Tibati (Tribune 2018 vows).
- **His words** (on the monastery renovation): "A well-planned novitiate tells young men entering the monastic life that they
  have a place, they matter and that the monks of the Abbey welcome and support them as they learn to give themselves totally
  to God in the monastery" (https://cullmantimes.com/2024/05/14/campaign-to-fund-st-bernard-monastery-upgrades-renovations/).
- **Feast day:** not stated.
- **Photo:** monk-img/joel-1.jpg.

## 5. Br. Charles Manning, O.S.B.

- **Abbey profile, in his own words [abbey]:**
  > I came to St. Bernard College from Knoxville, Tennessee, and loved the place. My math professor was Father John Capesius,
  > O.S.B. He was brilliant and the happiest man I had ever met. I like to say I was "charmed" by the elderly Father John to
  > enter the monastery. I knew the Sisters of Mercy growing up in school and at the hospital where my mother worked, and
  > admired them and the good work they did, so I knew what religious life was about.
  >
  > In 2021 I celebrated my golden jubilee of vows (fifty years) and I have to reflect and say it hasn't always been easy, but
  > it has been a good life. I smile to think that I took a vow of stability, but have traveled, lived, and worked so many
  > places as a Benedictine! After teaching some forty years, I now serve the community as treasurer ("procurator" in monk
  > talk) and consider myself blessed to still be active and productive.
- **Family / hometown:** Knoxville, Tennessee. Son of John J. Manning and Margaret Murray Manning, second of three sons.
  Knoxville Catholic High School, 1969 (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]).
- **Vows:** first vows 11 July 1971 (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]).
- **Education:** BA Biology, St. Bernard College, 1974. Physics at Loyola, New Orleans. MS in Aquatic Biology, University of
  Tennessee, 1978 (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]).
- **Work** (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]): Registrar in the last year of Southern Benedictine College, 1978–79. Taught sciences at
  St. Augustine's College, Nassau, Bahamas, 1979–80. "Returned to St. Bernard to open the St. Bernard Retreat Center in 1981."
  Diocesan School Board 1979–2018. "The first non-priest to be appointed Sub-prior of the Abbey by Abbot Hillary Dreaper." On
  the planning committee that reopened the Prep in 1983. Taught Biology and Physics there 1984–2018. Treasurer (Procurator)
  from 2019.
- **Crafts / outdoors:** "Over the years he has enjoyed working on the campus grounds and landscaping, planting a number of
  trees on campus" (Jubilees 2021 [abbey]).
- **Feast day:** not stated.
